Start here

Click Open photos and choose your store photos (the STORES files). The boxes the model found are drawn in automatically for those photos; you fix them and tag the brands.

What to do on each photo

  1. Tag brands: drag across a run of the same brand to select those boxes, then press its number key. Click a single box to pick just one. Use 9 Other for brands not in the list.
  2. Delete wrong boxes (shelf edges, price tags, duplicates): select them and press Del.
  3. Draw missing products: press D (draw mode), drag one box per visible product face, press D again to go back to selecting. Shift+drag also draws.
  4. When every product has a box and a brand, click Mark photo done.

Keys

  • 1…9 set brand of selected boxes (new boxes get the last brand used)
  • 0 clear brand · Del delete · Ctrl+Z undo
  • U select all untagged · A select all · Esc clear selection
  • D draw mode on/off · Ctrl+click or Ctrl+drag add to selection
  • +/− zoom · N/P next/previous photo
